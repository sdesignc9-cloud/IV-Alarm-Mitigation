About our project

We are developing an autonomous robotic system that detects and prevents downstream IV occlusions, reducing nursing workloads and improving patient care.

IV pump alarms frequently interrupt medical staff, contributing to alarm fatigue and taking valuable time away from patient care. Our system uses pressure monitoring to detect potential occlusions before they occur and deploys a robotic arm to automatically correct kinks in IV tubing. By minimizing manual intervention, our design aims to reduce unnecessary alarms, improve infusion reliability, and allow nurses to focus on more urgent patient needs.
