Conceptual Design

Design Overview

Design Concept 1

A Simple Rack and Pinion style gripper. With only one servo, it manages to grip simple objects, but is limited in maneuverability and accessibility.

Design Concept 2

A Linkage with complementary grippers and one servo, it manages to provide a stronger grip in a more maneuverable configuration, but is not gentle and can end up causing more problems than it solves.

Design Concept 3

A Crank Arm Linkage with complementary Swappable Grippers, and can be configured with one or two servos, it provides a strong grip in a maneuverable configuration.
